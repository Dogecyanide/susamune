Moonshine PR27 - safe starts and automatic drafts

Use PR27 on every participant. Online protocol 5 deliberately rejects PR26
because its Ready message does not guarantee a safe, reserved stage.

1. Ordinary IL race: choose Ready while the other player is loading/entering
   a stage. Nobody should warp early. Once each ready player reaches normal
   gameplay, their game waits on Online; both then depart exactly once.
2. Repeat with host loading, guest loading, a shine exit/save prompt, and an
   already-requested warp. Finish the current transition normally. A save
   prompt may need dismissing. The race must not replace that transition.
3. Cancel readiness with A or B while waiting. Neither player should become
   stuck; choose Ready again to start. Disconnect/reconnect while waiting too.
4. Draft: the IL list opens when your turn begins. B returns to Online without
   reopening every frame. It opens again on the next turn. Check both opening
   picks and counterpicks; an active editor/transition should defer the prompt.
5. Repeat readiness and draft tests with 3 and 4 players. The last loading
   player must hold up everyone. Each player bans once in the group draft.
6. Stageloader: safe initial start, then independent advancement through the
   host's playlist. Ordinary free roam must remain unpaused.
7. Retest PR26 smoothness on Wii/Wii and Wii/Dolphin, all three ghost models,
   level changes and reconnects. PR27 adds no further smoothing changes.

Host checks and fixed-timer builds are not substitutes for these Wii checks.
No timer formulas, savestate format, settings IDs or memory reservations change.
