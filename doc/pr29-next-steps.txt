PR29 focus: stable player slots and persistent rooms.

retries in place. A peer leaving or two minutes without it does not erase the room.
Listener retries retain the original guest endpoint, and group packets are routed
to their owner before being offered to a vacant slot. Group ghost updates use the
bounded queue with fair recipients instead of competing for one latest-pose slot.
Online row values fit within the space left by their labels.
