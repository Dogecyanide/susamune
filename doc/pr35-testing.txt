Moonshine 3.0.0 Timewarp - Prototype 35

New: community ghost downloads directly on Wii

Ghosts > Get ghosts > Delfino Ghosts opens the public community catalogue.
Choose a ghost and press A to download. R shows the next page; Y returns to
the newest page. B returns or cancels a pending request. Downloaded files
appear in Imported ghosts after leaving the online browser.
Recorded times appear beside each ghost to three decimal places. An unfinished
recording shows its duration and is marked '(clip)' below the title.

No account or PC companion is needed. This first native Wii build supports
browsing and downloading; account linking and uploads are still to follow.
The catalogue asks for ghosts playable on your current game region.
The Wii clock must be reasonably correct for secure HTTPS connections.

Each download is checked against the advertised size and SHA-256, then the
ordinary Moonshine ghost validator. Incomplete files are not imported and
existing ghosts are not replaced. A temporary download uses the existing
transfer workspace, so saving/loading states and other ghost transfers may
report busy until it finishes or cancellation is acknowledged.

Install the matching launcher and all regional mod files together.
Standalone Dolphin builds continue to use the website on the PC.
No custom music, photo, account token or personal data is in this package.

Wii checks needed
1. Open the catalogue on a Wii with working internet; browse and turn a page.
2. Download a ghost, return, select Imported ghosts, and watch it.
3. Download that same ghost again: the existing copy must remain intact.
4. Cancel while connecting/downloading, then try again. The menu must remain
   responsive; other storage may remain busy until IOS completes its request.
5. Check ordinary gameplay/ISO loading after leaving the catalogue.

The PC checks cover production TLS against the real public service, HTTP/JSON
parsing, IOS request layouts and cancellation ownership, and temporary-file
validation/publication. They do not establish real Wii connectivity or speed.
Four-player online stability still requires the separate hardware session.
