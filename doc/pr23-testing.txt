Moonshine 3.0.0 Timewarp - Prototype 23

Use the Moonshine Prototype 23 app. Existing settings and recordings stay in
their usual locations. This package includes JP, US and PAL support.

Priority checks

1. Guidebook: the three visible categories should keep equal widths as you
   switch tabs. Icons and labels are slightly larger. Check the hand points
   at the middle of the first letter without touching it. Boo and sunglasses
   should alternate small held poses. Try the longest category names. The
   sidebar should use the native level-name banner above its existing
   postcard. Check the name matches the world, including Gelato and a
   secret stage belonging to another world.

2. Displays & styles: choose Timers, Inputs & movement, HUD & messages, or
   Custom text before choosing an item. Check that Back returns to its group
   and Search still opens the correct item. Per-item editors retain their
   combined visibility, Position & size, Colours and Animation controls.

3. Sunshine timer: switch Decimal separator between Colon and Dot. Check
   the normal three-decimal timer at normal and edited sizes, a gradient,
   then save settings and relaunch. Piantissimo/blooper native race timers
   should retain their original colon and two-decimal layout.

4. Ricco Free camera: pan and turn around the giant cage, boats and signs,
   then stop moving the camera. Check whether the wobble is gone and whether
   movement stays smooth. Also close Free camera and check ordinary gameplay.
   This synchronization change is a candidate fix needing Wii confirmation.

5. Ghost camera: record a short ghost, then Watch with Recorded camera and
   Free camera. Enter Mario/FLUDD colours while watching and verify the model
   preview wins while open; leaving the editor should restore Watch camera.

6. Open the Warp Wheel over a retail save prompt. A/B and movement must
   operate only the wheel, including the press that closes it. After closing,
   the prompt should respond normally to a fresh press.

7. Practice > Gameplay > World rules > Sirena pineapple: in Plaza, switch
   Present/Removed, check visibility and pipe collision, then reset normally.
   Sirena Enter IL should still apply its own starting setup. This edits the
   current game flag; it is not an always-on override of an IL.

8. Creation, text keyboards, numeric entry and breakdown options should share
   the parchment, gold/green selection and animated native icons. Check light
   and dark menu colours, selected-text readability, and Keep/Cancel.
   The current colour target (for example Gloves) is larger, with an X Next
   item hint beside it. X changes the target; START keeps its Save function.

The L/R category buttons now have green backing and gold trim; check their
contrast against your menu background colour.
PAL/US Shined markers should be shines rather than sticks. Keyboard Cancel
should show X & START, without an extra X icon. Percentage fields in Creation
and number entry use pct because the native font's percent slot is a C button.
Small header icons should stay inside their green bar in both poses.

Short regression pass

Save/load all three slots and overwrite one occupied slot. Check one hidden
HUD preview and ordinary controls after closing an editor. Please report
region and the last action if anything freezes.

Remaining validation

The Ricco synchronization change passed a limited emulator run without a
freeze; a complete visual fix is not yet confirmed. Online transport is
unchanged; the larger network test can wait until testers are available.
