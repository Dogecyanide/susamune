Moonshine 3.0.0 Timewarp PR44

Changes
- Online characters' water uses their online visibility setting, matching their
  bodies. Ordinary recorded-ghost visibility no longer hides online spray.
- An empty host room can change its race setup before the first guest connects.
  The first handshake sends those options. Existing matches remain protected
  during temporary disconnects; reconnecting does not silently replace a race.
- Native Dolphin DNS groundwork for Delfino Ghosts. The website browser and
  persistent ghost/state/TAS files are still pending on standalone Dolphin.

Checks
- Focused race/lifecycle and ghost-water/render checks pass.
- Native Ethernet/UDP/DNS tests include wrong senders, malformed compression,
  aliases, timeouts, cancellation and unchanged online packet delivery.
- All regional builds and protected timer/layout checks are required by the
  release packager. See its receipt for the exact source/build scope.

Hardware follow-up
1. With ordinary Ghost visible off, verify online water in both directions.
2. Create an empty room, change its mode/options, then connect a guest and
   confirm the selected setup. A brief disconnect during a race must retain it.
3. Four-player hardware acceptance remains outstanding. PR43 two-player native
   Dolphin/Wii crossplay and Wii Search were accepted by the user; do not treat
   that as validation of every mode, platform, or new PR44 binary.

Personal themes, account links and recordings are excluded from shared ZIPs.
