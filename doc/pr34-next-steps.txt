Storage notes
Named codes fill the existing 224-byte configuration gap at 0x1F20..0x2000; no MEM1/MEM2 reservation grows. The kernel initializes this PPC-owned block before release and reads it only during an ordinary settings save. Older kernels have no contacts magic and fail closed. Each region retains its own creation-section keys. Names are bounded to 15 visible ASCII characters and eight entries. Remembering peers does not change invitation slots or initiate a connection.
