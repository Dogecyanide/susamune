PAL live memory observations, 2026-10-28

Six complete 19-record snapshots: Plaza, Noki 8, Bianco 5, Bianco 3 secret,
Sirena 1 with split manta (user reported), and Noki 6 secret while falling
(user reported). Capture metadata cannot independently prove actor/action state.
All capture completeness flags are clear. Exact records and hashes are in
build/pr48-live-memory/capture-comparison.json and its referenced source dumps.

Every snapshot has four leaf audio allocations, each identified through the
retail type and name getter layout. Every allocation size equals its matching
PAL disc AudioRes/Banks file size. Three banks are shared by all six captures:
w1stLoad_0.aw 64,512; wScene_16.aw 3,844,736; w2ndLoad_0.aw 11,363,584 bytes.
Their sum is 15,272,832 bytes (14.565 MiB). A fourth scene bank changes by area.
These are allocated file-sized blocks, not an empty nested heap reservation.
This does not establish which sounds are played or how much padding a bank has.
No raw sound-bank payload was captured from the Wii.

The two nonempty HUD/guidebook archive blocks total 329,152 bytes in every
snapshot. Their allocator leaves a 440,896-byte (430.5625 KiB) trailing gap at
Wii 0x90F945C0..0x91000000. This is an additional reclamation candidate, not a
memory grant. Trace other ARAM allocator users, regional asset extents and
reset/savestate lifetimes before reserving it. Any reservation must use the
allocator or formally reduce its extent; writing into its free list is unsafe.

The root MEM1 heap reserves child heaps, explaining its near-full display.
Bianco 5 uses 14,157,044 bytes of the 14,942,176-byte stage heap, leaving 785,132.
The split-manta snapshot is smaller than Bianco 5; secrets use considerably less.
A stable allocated size is expected when game objects are preallocated or pooled.
These snapshots do not prove global peak or transient allocation bounds.

The Noki 6 secret's physical area is 0x1F (internal RicoEx1); the PC label was
incorrectly Ricco secret. Raw area IDs in the saved evidence are correct.
The monitor source/UI label is corrected; the active receiver uses its current
in-memory labels until its next restart. No recapture is needed.
