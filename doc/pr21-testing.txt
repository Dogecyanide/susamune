MOONSHINE 3.0.0 TIMEWARP - PROTOTYPE 21

Install the included apps folder on the SD. Keep your personal settings/data.
All game regions are included. Japanese-menu ZIP differs only in UI language.

Focused hardware checks
- Search: open, enter a broad and a narrow query, page results and open one.
- Displays and styles: choose an item, toggle it, open Edit appearance.
  Choose Position, Size, Colours or Animation with A. B returns to the item.
  Save changes keeps the look; Discard changes restores it. Reopen/reboot.
- Menu background: select Original guidebook or Custom inside its editor.
- Try Flow, Pulse, Ping-pong and Wave for QFT, Sunshine timer and custom text.
- Sunshine timer: 3 decimal places for Moonshine timing, 2 for native races.
  Check Piantissimo and blooper races while running AND on their final result.
  Restart/leave the race, then check normal IL timing returns to 3 decimals.
  Check pause, Step, slowdown, savestate restore, scale and colour/outline.

Known remaining issue
- The reported Ricco free-camera wobble on signs, the cage and boats is still
  under investigation. PR21 does not claim to fix it.

Online roadmap: four-player rooms and stageloader races remain planned.
Friends, accounts and ratings are no longer planned for this release.
